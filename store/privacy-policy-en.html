Effective: September 15, 2026　 · 　Last updated: September 15, 2026

One-line summary

VoiceType is a fully offline speech-to-text app. Your audio, transcripts, and exported files are processed entirely on your device — they are never uploaded to any server, and no third-party services are contacted.

What we do not collect

  • No audio, transcripts, exported files, or any user content.
  • No device identifiers, location, contacts, cookies, or account info.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, social, or push SDKs.
  • No accounts, sign-up, or login flows.
  • No ads, tracking, or A/B testing.

Where your data lives

Transcript history and preferences are stored only in localStorage on your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything; nothing syncs to a cloud service or device-vendor backup.

On Android we explicitly disable allowBackup so the auto-backup mechanism does not silently copy your transcripts to your cloud storage — that would contradict the "data stays on this device" promise.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions strictly required for its core feature — no optional or fallback permissions:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): used only when you tap the record button. The OS will prompt for your consent. Audio streams are released immediately after inference; they are never written to disk, cached, or uploaded.
  • External storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and below): used only when you actively choose "Save as .txt / .html" — writes one file to your device's Downloads folder. Android 10+ does not need this permission.

The INTERNET permission is kept for compatibility with the WebView's same-origin checks; the app itself never makes a network request for speech recognition.

Third parties

No third-party SDK, ad, analytics, or tracking component is bundled. The offline runtimes (whisper.cpp / onnxruntime / transformers.js) are open-source and shipped inside the app; they do not contact upstream services at runtime.

Children

The app is not designed for children under 13, but because it collects no data at all, it is equally safe for any age.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be surfaced in-app and require your re-confirmation.
